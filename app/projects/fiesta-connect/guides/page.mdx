# Guides

**Fiesta Connect** — How-to guides and tutorials

---

## Getting Started

Welcome to the Fiesta Connect guides. These how-to articles will help you navigate the app and make the most of everything it has to offer.

---

## How to Create an Account

1. Download Fiesta Connect from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your name, email address, and a secure password.
4. Verify your email and complete your profile setup.

---

## How to Discover Events

1. Open the **Events** tab from the home screen.
2. Browse upcoming events or use the search bar to find a specific event.
3. Tap any event to view details, location, and participating vendors.

---

## How to RSVP to an Event

1. Open the event you want to attend.
2. Tap the **RSVP** button.
3. Confirm your attendance — a confirmation will be sent to your registered email.

---

## Troubleshooting

**I'm not receiving confirmation emails.**
Check your spam or junk folder. Ensure the email address on your account is correct.

**The event map isn't loading.**
Make sure location permissions are enabled for Fiesta Connect in your device settings.

**I can't log in.**
Tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen and follow the reset instructions sent to your email.
