# Privacy Policy

**Fiesta Connect** — Last updated: March 9, 2026

**By Rogelio Zamora Jr | .Enterface, LLC**

---

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how .Enterface, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Fiesta Connect application ("App").

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- Account registration details (name, email address)
- Community and event interaction data
- Any content you submit through the App

### Information Collected Automatically
- Device identifiers and operating system information
- App usage data and interaction logs
- Location data (only when you grant permission) to show nearby event information

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Operate and improve the Fiesta Connect App
- Deliver event schedules, performer info, and vendor listings
- Send you push notifications about announcements and event updates (with your consent)
- Respond to your support requests

---

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:
- **Service providers** (e.g., Firebase) that help us operate the App
- **Law enforcement** when required by applicable law

---

## Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

---

## Children's Privacy

The Fiesta Connect App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App.
