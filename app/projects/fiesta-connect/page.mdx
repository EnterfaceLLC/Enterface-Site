# Fiesta Connect

**Fiesta Connect** is a mobile app designed to digitally connect the Topeka community with the cultural tradition of Fiesta Mexicana — celebrating heritage, culture, and community in one place.

---

## The Vision

Fiesta Mexicana is one of Topeka's most beloved cultural events. Fiesta Connect was built to give that tradition a digital home — making it easier for attendees to engage with the event, discover vendors and performers, and stay connected to the culture year-round.

---

## Key Features

- **Event guide** — schedules, performers, and vendors all in one place
- **Cultural highlights** — stories and info about the traditions celebrated at Fiesta Mexicana
- **Community connection** — bridging the gap between the digital world and in-person cultural events
- **Notifications** — stay up to date on announcements and event changes

---

## Tech Stack

- **React Native** for cross-platform iOS & Android
- **Firebase** for real-time data and push notifications
- **Node.js** backend for event and vendor management
