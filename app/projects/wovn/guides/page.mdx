# Guides

**WOVN** — How-to guides and tutorials

## Getting Started

Welcome to the WOVN guides. These how-to articles are here to help you navigate the platform and get the most out of supporting your local economy.

---

## How to Create an Account

1. Download WOVN from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your details and choose whether you're joining as a **Community Member** or a **Local Business**.
4. Verify your email to activate your account.

---

## How to Discover Local Businesses

1. Open the **Explore** tab on the home screen.
2. Browse businesses by category or use the search bar.
3. Tap any business to view their profile, offerings, and contact information.

---

## How to Support a Local Business

1. Find a business you want to support using the **Explore** tab.
2. Tap **Support** to follow their updates, share their profile, or make a purchase.
3. The more you engage, the stronger your local network grows.

---

## Troubleshooting

**The app isn't loading business listings.**
Ensure you have an active internet connection and try pulling down to refresh.

**I'm not receiving verification emails.**
Check your spam or junk folder. Confirm that your account email is correct in **Settings**.

**I can't log in.**
Tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen and follow the reset instructions.
