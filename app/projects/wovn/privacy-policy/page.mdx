# Privacy Policy

**WOVN** — Last updated: March 9, 2026

**By Rogelio Zamora Jr | .Enterface, LLC**  
**In collaboration with Primitive Path Consulting**

---

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how .Enterface, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the WOVN application ("App").

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- Account registration details (name, email address, business information)
- Entrepreneur profiles, stories, and product or service listings
- Community interactions and posts within the App

### Information Collected Automatically
- Device identifiers and operating system information
- App usage data and interaction logs

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Operate and improve the WOVN platform
- Display entrepreneur profiles and connect community members with local businesses
- Send you notifications about platform updates and community activity (with your consent)
- Respond to your support requests

---

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information you choose to make public in your entrepreneur profile (name, business name, story, products) is visible to other App users. We may also share information with:
- **Service providers** that help us operate the App
- **Law enforcement** when required by applicable law

---

## Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

---

## Children's Privacy

The WOVN App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App.
