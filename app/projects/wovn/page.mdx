# WOVN

> Building a safe and inclusive digital platform for our local entrepreneurs to connect, share their stories, and grow together

**WOVN** is a community-driven mobile platform designed to highlight the people behind local businesses.
Our mission is to create a space where entrepreneurs feel seen, supported, and connected, while helping community members discover and intentionally support local talent.
At its core, WOVN is about strengthening the fabric of our local economy by weaving together stories, products, and people into a shared digital ecosystem.

---

# Our Vision

Local entrepreneurs are more than the products they sell, they are artists, builders, creators, and leaders within our communities.

**WOVN exists to provide a safe and welcoming digital environment** where entrepreneurs can:

* Share their authentic stories
* Showcase their products and services
* Connect with supportive community members
* Grow within a local ecosystem built on trust and collaboration

Through thoughtful design and intentional community engagement, **WOVN** aims to encourage mindful spending and stronger local support networks.

---

# Project Goals

## Community Vision (Monique)

> To create an app space where people and businesses can stay connected more authentically.

**WOVN** will encourage mindful shopping, meaningful relationships, and deeper community support.

## Development Vision (.Enterface)

> To develop and launch a digital hub that connects and amplifies local entrepreneurs by showcasing their brand identities, personal stories, and the products they create.

**WOVN** aims to strengthen local ecosystems by:

* Promoting intentional spending within the community
* Increasing visibility for entrepreneurs
* Creating opportunities for collaboration and support

___

# Why WOVN?

Traditional platforms often focus on transactions and algorithms.

We believe that when communities understand the stories behind the businesses around them, they are more likely to:
* Support local entrepreneurs
* Invest in their local economy
* Build stronger community connections

**WOVN exists to help weave those connections together**.
