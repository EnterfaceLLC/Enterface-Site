---

# Project Roots

Rooted in community values and guided by ancestral knowledge, this Indigenous-operated consulting business works to strengthen cultural awareness, grow meaningful allyship, and open new pathways to entrepreneurship through an Indigenous lens. Our approach centers reciprocity, shared learning, and relationships built on respect rather than extraction.


# Project Goals

**Monique:** To create an app space where people and businesses can stay connected more authentically. This app will hopefully encourage more mindful shopping and community support.*

**Enterface:** To develop and launch the app a digital hub that connects and amplifies local entrepreneurs by showcasing their brand identity (stories) and their products. This app aims to create a supportive local ecosystem that ultimately strengthens intentional spending.
