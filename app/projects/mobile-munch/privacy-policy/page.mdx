# Privacy Policy

**mobile Munch** — Last updated: March 9, 2026

**By Rogelio Zamora Jr | .Enterface, LLC**

---

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how .Enterface, LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the mobile Munch application ("App" or "Service"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. **We do not sell your personal information to anyone.**

---

## Interpretation and Definitions

### Interpretation
The words of which the initial letter is capitalized have meanings defined below.

### Definitions
- **Account:** A unique account created for you to access our Service.
- **Application (or App):** Refers to mobile Munch, the software program provided by the Company.
- **Company (referred to as "We," "Us," or "Our"):** Refers to .Enterface, LLC, 4827 SW Misty Harbor Ave.
- **Device:** Any device that can access the Service such as a cellphone.
- **Personal Data:** Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
- **Service Provider:** Any third-party company that processes data on behalf of the Company to facilitate the Service.
- **You:** The individual accessing or using the Service.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** When you register, we collect your name and email address.
- **Rewards Activity:** Check-in history and Munch Rewards points balance.
- **Communications:** Any content you submit when contacting support.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information:** Device identifiers, IP address, operating system, and mobile browser type.
- **Usage Data:** How you interact with the App (e.g., features used, time spent).
- **Location Data:** We collect precise or approximate location information **only if you grant permission** through your device settings. This is used to show nearby food trucks and verify check-ins for rewards.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- **Operate and improve** the mobile Munch App
- **Manage your Account** and Munch Rewards points
- **Find local vendors** near you (requires location permission)
- **Verify check-ins** for the reward system
- **Send notifications** about nearby food trucks and events (with your consent)
- **Respond to your support requests**
- **Analyze usage trends** to enhance app performance

---

## No Sale of Data

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.** Your data is used solely to operate and improve the mobile Munch App.

---

## Sharing Your Information

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

- **With Service Providers:** Trusted third parties (e.g., AWS cloud hosting) who help us operate the App, provided they agree to keep your data confidential.
- **With Vendors:** When you check-in at a vendor location, necessary information (such as your identity for reward redemption) may be visible to the vendor.
- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred. If this occurs, we will ensure the acquiring party honors this Privacy Policy.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

---

## Data Retention

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. You may request deletion of your account at any time.

Specifically:
- **Account Information:** Retained while your account is active.
- **Usage Data:** Retained for a shorter period for internal analysis, unless required for security or legal compliance.

---

## Your Rights and Control

- **Access and Correction:** You may update or correct your account information by signing into the App.
- **Location Permissions:** You can enable or disable location services at any time through your device settings. *Note: Disabling location may prevent core features (like finding nearby trucks) from working.*
- **Deletion:** You can request deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the email below. We will comply unless we have a legal obligation to retain it.

---

## Children's Privacy

The mobile Munch App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us immediately.

---

## Security

The security of your Personal Data is important to us. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect it, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
