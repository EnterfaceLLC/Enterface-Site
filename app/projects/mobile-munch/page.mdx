# mobile Munch
*version 4.0.0*

**mobile Munch** is Topeka's number ONE food truck finder. Its a mobile app built to help foodies discover and explore the best food trucks in Topeka, Kansas and beyond!

---

## The Problem

Attending a food truck festival or event meant walking stall to stall just to figure out what was available. Menus were hard to read, lines were long, and decisions were rushed. There had to be a better way.

---

## The Solution

A simple, clean mobile app that puts every food truck's full menu in your pocket — so you can plan your meal before you ever get in line.

Key features:
- **Browse menus** for all participating food trucks
- **Check-in** at your favorite trucks to earn Munch Rewards points
- **Discover** trucks at upcoming events and festivals around Topeka
- **Live updates** so menus stay current
