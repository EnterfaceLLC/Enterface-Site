# Guides

**mobile Munch** — How-to guides and tutorials

**By Rogelio Zamora Jr | .Enterface, LLC**

---

## Getting Started

Welcome to the mobile Munch guides. These how-to articles are here to help you get the most out of the app — whether you're a first-time user or a returning foodie.

---

## How to Find a Food Truck

1. Open the mobile Munch app.
2. Browse the **Food Trucks** tab to see all participating trucks.
3. Tap any truck to view their full menu, location, and hours.

---

## How to Earn Munch Rewards Points

1. Navigate to a food truck's page inside the app.
2. Tap **Check-In** when you are at the truck's location.
3. Points are automatically added to your Munch Rewards balance after a successful check-in.

---

## How to Redeem Rewards

1. Go to the **Rewards** tab in the app.
2. Review your available point balance.
3. Select a reward tier and follow the on-screen instructions to redeem at a participating truck.

---

## Troubleshooting

**The app won't load menus.**
Make sure you have an active internet connection. Pull down on the screen to refresh.

**Check-in is not working.**
Verify that location permissions are enabled for mobile Munch in your device settings.

**I forgot my password.**
Tap **Forgot Password** on the login screen and follow the email reset instructions.
