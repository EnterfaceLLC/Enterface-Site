## Welcome to mobile Munch Rewards!

Earn free food by checking in at your favorite food truck events! This guide explains how the rewards program works for both users and vendors.

---

## For Users

### What is the Rewards Program?

The mobile Munch Rewards Program lets you earn punches by visiting participating food trucks at their events. Collect **6 verified visits** with a vendor and redeem a **free reward**!

### How It Works

**Step 1: Find Participating Vendors**

1. Open the **My Rewards** screen from the app navigation
2. View all participating vendors in the Rewards Program
3. Each vendor card displays:

   * Vendor name and logo
   * Current punch card progress (e.g., 4/6)
   * Active event information (if available)
   * A Digital Punch Card showing earned visits

---

**Step 2: Earn Punches with the Digital Punch Card**

Instead of a traditional progress bar, mobile Munch uses a **Digital Punch Card** system.

* Each participating vendor has a punch card containing **6 punch slots**
* Every successful check-in fills one slot with the vendor's logo
* A subtle animation confirms each successful punch
* Complete all **6 punches** to unlock a reward

The Digital Punch Card provides a visual representation of your progress and resets after a successful reward redemption.

---

**Step 3: Attend Active Events**

***Important***: You can only check in when a vendor has an **active event**!

* Look for vendors showing **"Active Now!"**
* Vendors without active events will show **"No active events. Check back later!"**
* Check the event banner on each vendor card for details
* Events are tied to specific locations and times

---

**Step 4: Enable Location Services**

Before checking in, you'll need to grant location permissions.

**Why?** Location verification ensures you're physically attending the event, preventing fraud and maintaining fairness for all users.

**How to Enable**:

* iOS: Settings → mobile Munch → Location → While Using the App
* Android: Settings → Apps → mobile Munch → Permissions → Location

---

**Step 5: Check In at Events**

When you arrive at an active event:

1. Tap the **"Check In"** button on the vendor card
2. The app verifies you're within proximity of the event location
3. If successful, you'll see: **"Check-In Successful!"** ✅
4. One punch is added to your Digital Punch Card immediately

**Check-In Rules**:

* ✅ Must be at an active event
* ✅ Must be within proximity of the event location
* ✅ Can only check in once per 24 hours per vendor
* ✅ Each successful visit earns one punch
* ✅ Events are tied to specific locations and times

**What if I'm too far away?**

* You'll see: **"Too Far Away - You must be at an active event to check in"**
* Move closer to the vendor's location and try again
* Verify you're attending the correct event location

**What if I already checked in recently?**

* You'll see: **"Already Checked In - You've already checked in recently. Check back for a future event!"**
* Wait 24 hours before checking in again with that vendor

---

**Step 6: Track Your Progress**

Monitor your rewards journey directly from the vendor card.

#### Digital Punch Card

* Each successful check-in fills one punch slot
* Vendor logos appear in completed slots
* Progress updates immediately after each successful visit
* Complete all **6 punches** to unlock a reward

**Examples:**

* 1/6 Visits Completed
* 3/6 Visits Completed
* 5/6 Visits Completed
* 6/6 Visits Completed — **Reward Ready! 🎉**

#### All-Time Statistics

You can also track:

* Total check-ins across all vendors
* Total rewards redeemed
* Overall rewards activity

---

**Step 7: Redeem Your Reward**

Once you reach **6 completed visits** with a vendor:

1. The **"Check In"** button changes to **"Redeem Reward"**
2. Tap **"Redeem Reward"**
3. You must be attending an active event
4. You must be within proximity of the event location
5. Confirm the redemption

**What Happens Next**:

* A 15-minute countdown timer starts
* You're taken to the redemption screen
* Show the screen to the vendor
* Receive your reward

---

**Step 8: Use Your Reward (15-Minute Timer)**

The redemption screen displays:

* **Countdown Timer:** 15:00 → 0:00
* **Vendor Information:** Name, logo, and event details
* **Redemption Status:** Active reward information

#### Timer Colors

* 🟢 Green: More than 5 minutes remaining
* 🟠 Orange: Between 2 and 5 minutes remaining
* 🔴 Red: Less than 2 minutes remaining

**To Complete Redemption**:

1. Show the screen to the vendor
2. Tap **"Mark as Redeemed"** after receiving your reward
3. Your Digital Punch Card resets to 0/6
4. Begin earning punches again

**If Timer Expires**:

* Redemption automatically expires at **0:00**
* Your punch card resets
* You'll need to earn **6 new visits**
* No penalties — simply start a new reward cycle

#### Active Redemption Indicator

If you leave the screen, you'll see:

> Active Redemption: X:XX Remaining

Tap **"Continue Redemption"** to return to the redemption screen.

Only one active redemption per vendor at a time.
