## Welcome to mobile Munch Rewards!

Earn free food by checking in at your favorite food truck events! This guide explains how the rewards program works for both users and vendors.

---

## For Users

### What is the Rewards Program?

The mobile Munch Rewards Program lets you earn points by visiting participating food trucks at their events. Collect **10 check-ins** with a vendor and redeem a **free item** of your choice!

### How It Works

**Step 1: Find Participating Vendors**

1. Open the **My Rewards** screen from the app navigation
2. View all participating vendors in the rewards program
3. Each vendor card shows:
   - Vendor name and logo
   - Current check-in progress (e.g., 3/10)
   - Active event information (if available)
   - Progress bar showing how close you are to a reward

---

**Step 2: Attend Active Events**

***Important***: You can only check in when a vendor has an **active event**!

- Look for vendors showing "Event Name - Active Now!"
- Vendors without active events will show "No active events. Check back later!"
- Check the event banner on each vendor card for event details
- Events are tied to specific locations and times

---

**Step 3: Enable Location Services**

Before checking in, you'll need to grant location permissions:

**Why?** Location verification ensures you're actually at the event, preventing fraud and maintaining fairness for all users.

**How to enable**:
- iOS: Settings → mobile Munch → Location → While Using the App
- Android: Settings → Apps → mobile Munch → Permissions → Location

---

**Step 4: Check In at Events**

When you arrive at an active event:

1. Tap the **"Check In"** button on the vendor's card
2. The app verifies you're within proximity of the event location
3. If successful, you'll see: "**Check-in Successful!** ✅"
4. Your progress bar updates immediately (e.g., 4/10)

**Check-in Rules**:
- ✅ Must be at an active event (between start and end time)
- ✅ Must be within proximity of the event location
- ✅ Can only check in once per 24 hours per vendor
- ✅ Event-based: Each event is a new opportunity to check in

**What if I'm too far away?**
- You'll see: "Too Far Away - You must be at an active event to check in"
- Move closer to the vendor's location and try again
- Make sure you're at the correct event location (some vendors serve at multiple spots)

**What if I already checked in recently?**
- You'll see: "Already Checked In - You've already checked in at this vendor event. Check back for future events!"
- Wait 24 hours before checking in again at that vendor

---

**Step 5: Track Your Progress**

Monitor your rewards journey:

**On Vendor Cards**:
- Visual progress bar (e.g., 70% filled = 7/10)
- Check-in counter (e.g., "7/10")
- Active event information

**All-Time Statistics**:
- Total check-ins across all vendors
- Total rewards redeemed
- View your achievements in the stats banner

---

**Step 6: Redeem Your Reward**

Once you reach **10 check-ins** with a vendor:

1. The **"Check In"** button changes to **"Redeem Reward"**
2. Tap **"Redeem Reward"**
3. You must be at an **active event** to redeem
4. You must be within **proximity** of the event location (larger radius than check-in)
5. Confirm the redemption

**What happens next**:
- A 15-minute countdown timer starts
- You're taken to the redemption screen
- Show this screen to the vendor
- Vendor provides your free item

---

**Step 7: Use Your Reward (15-Minute Timer)**

The redemption screen shows:
- **Countdown Timer**: 15:00 → 0:00 (changes color as time runs out)
  - Green: More than 5 minutes remaining
  - Orange: 2-5 minutes remaining
  - Red: Less than 2 minutes remaining
- **Vendor Information**: Name, logo, event location
- **Redemption Details**: What you're redeeming and when

**To Complete Redemption**:
1. Show the screen to the vendor
2. Tap "Mark as Redeemed" after receiving your item
3. Your check-ins reset to 0/10
4. Start collecting points again!

**If Timer Expires**:
- Redemption automatically expires at **0:00**
- Your check-ins are still reset (**can't spam the system**)
- You'll need to earn 10 new check-ins
- No penalties - just start fresh!

**Active Redemption Indicator**:
- If you leave the screen, you'll see **"Active redemption: X:XX remaining"**
- Tap **"Continue Redemption"** to return to the redemption screen
- Only one active redemption per vendor at a time
