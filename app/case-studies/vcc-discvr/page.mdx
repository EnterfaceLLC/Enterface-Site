# DISCVR

**DISCVR** is a modern cross-platform mobile application being developed for **Victory City Church** in Topeka, KS. Designed as the church's digital hub, the app allows members and visitors to discover weekly sermons via audio playback, reference sermon notes, and stay connected with the life of the church.

Inspired by the simplicity of modern media streaming apps, DISCVR delivers an intuitive sermon listening experience while laying the foundation for future church engagement features.

---

# Vision

Victory City Church desired a single destination where members and guests could easily access sermons, stay informed about church events, and engage with ministries throughout the week, not just on Sundays.

Rather than relying on multiple disconnected platforms, the goal is to provide one unified mobile experience that helps people:

- **Discover** new teachings, ministries, and events
- **Grow** through biblical teaching and sermon resources
- **Belong** by staying connected with the Victory City Church community

---

# The Problem

Church members often rely on social media posts, YouTube links, websites, printed notes, and emails to stay informed. Important content is spread across multiple platforms, making it difficult to find past sermons or remain engaged throughout the week.

There was an opportunity to create a dedicated mobile experience that keeps the church community connected wherever they are.

---

# The Solution

DISCVR centralizes Victory City Church's weekly content into a clean, modern mobile application focused on accessibility and ease of use.

The MVP focuses on delivering an exceptional sermon experience while establishing the technical foundation for future expansion.

### MVP Features

- **Music-inspired sermon audio player**
- **Complete sermon catalog**
- **Full sermon notes and scripture references**
- **Series organization**
- **Featured sermons**
- **Push notifications**
- **Speaker information**
- **Cross-platform support for iOS and Android**
- **Modern, intuitive user experience**

# Technical Highlights

The application is being built using a modern cloud-native architecture designed for scalability and real-time synchronization.

### Frontend

- **React Native**
- **TypeScript**

### Backend

- **Convex** for real-time backend and database
- **Clerk Authentication**
- **AWS S3 + CloudFront** for sermon audio and media delivery

### Features

- **Real-time content synchronization**
- **Secure authentication**
- **Cloud-hosted sermon media**
- **Responsive cross-platform UI**
- **Offline-ready architecture (planned)**

---

# Design Philosophy

The user experience is heavily inspired by modern media streaming applications.

Key design principles include:

- Clean, distraction-free interface
- Rich artwork and media presentation
- Fast navigation
- Simple audio controls
- Beautiful typography
- Accessible design
- Consistent church branding

The result is an experience that feels familiar to users while remaining focused on the church's mission of helping people discover, grow, and belong.
