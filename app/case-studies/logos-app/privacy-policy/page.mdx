# Privacy Policy

**ΛΟΓΟΣ (Logos)** — Last updated: April 3, 2026

**By Rogelio Zamora Jr | .Enterface, LLC**

---

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how .Enterface, LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the ΛΟΓΟΣ (Logos) application ("App" or "Service").

Logos is an **experimental prototype** currently in active development. As a prototype, the App's data practices are minimal. We are committed to being transparent about what information, if any, is collected.

**We do not sell your personal information to anyone.**

---

## Interpretation and Definitions

### Interpretation
The words of which the initial letter is capitalized have meanings defined below.

### Definitions
- **Application (or App):** Refers to ΛΟΓΟΣ (Logos), the experimental mobile app provided by the Company.
- **Company (referred to as "We," "Us," or "Our"):** Refers to .Enterface, LLC.
- **Device:** Any device that can access the App such as a cellphone or tablet.
- **Personal Data:** Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
- **Prototype:** A working model of the App intended for testing and iterative design — not a final production release.
- **You:** The individual accessing or using the App.

---

## Prototype Status

Logos is a **prototype** and is not publicly distributed on the App Store or Google Play at this time. Access is limited to testers, collaborators, and individuals participating in demos or feedback sessions.

Because of this limited scope, active data collection is minimal. This policy will be updated as the App evolves toward a public release.

---

## Information We Collect

### During Prototype Testing
At this stage, we may collect limited information to support development and feedback, including:

- **Feedback Submissions:** Any comments, bug reports, or suggestions you voluntarily provide.
- **Device Information:** Basic device type, operating system, and app version for debugging purposes.
- **Usage Patterns:** Anonymous interaction data (e.g., screens visited, features used) to inform design decisions.

### Information We Do Not Currently Collect
- We do not require account creation or collect email addresses at this stage.
- We do not collect precise location data.
- We do not collect payment or financial information.

---

## How We Use This Information

Any information collected during prototype testing is used solely to:

- **Improve the App** — Identify bugs, usability issues, and design improvements
- **Understand Engagement** — Learn which features resonate and which need refinement
- **Respond to Feedback** — Follow up on tester-submitted questions or reports

---

## No Sale of Data

**We do not sell, rent, or trade any information to third parties.** Data collected during prototype testing is used exclusively to develop and improve the Logos App.

---

## Sharing Your Information

We do not share tester information with third parties except in the following limited cases:

- **Development Tools:** We may use trusted third-party services (e.g., crash reporting or analytics tools) strictly to support app development. These providers are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.
- **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

---

## Data Retention

Any data collected during prototype testing is retained only as long as necessary to support active development. Upon a full product release or formal sunsetting of the prototype, collected data will be reviewed and purged in accordance with applicable law.

---

## Children's Privacy

The Logos App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

---

## Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect any information collected during testing. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App develops. We will reflect changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
