# Rogelio Zamora Jr.


**Founder & Full Stack Developer | .Enterface, LLC**

---

## About

Rogelio is the founder of **.Enterface, LLC**, a Topeka-based startup specializing in mobile apps, web apps, and design systems. Driven by a passion for building things that matter, he combines technical depth with a user-first mindset to create products that are functional, polished, and a joy to use.

---

## What He Does

- Architects and develops cross-platform mobile apps using **React Native**
- Builds full-stack web applications with **Next.js**, **Node.js**, **Supabase** and **AWS**
- Leads product design and UX across all .Enterface projects
- Manages the full development lifecycle — from idea to App Store
- Leads digital art branding and design initiatives
