# Jorge F. Hernandez

**Lead Cinematographer | .Enterface, LLC**

---

## About

Jorge is the Lead Cinematographer at **.Enterface, LLC**, bringing a sharp creative eye and a passion for visual storytelling to every project. He is the visual voice behind the brand, capturing the energy of the Topeka community and the spirit of .Enterface's work through film and photography.

---

## What He Does

- Leads all video production and cinematography for .Enterface projects
- Creates compelling visual content for app launches, social media, and marketing
- Documents events including food truck festivals, community gatherings, and product demos
- Collaborates closely with the development team to align visuals with the product experience
