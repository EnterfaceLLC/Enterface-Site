# 2025 Food Truck Season Kickoff -> First Photoshoot Session

The 2025 food truck season is officially underway and we kicked it off with our **first dedicated photoshoot session** focused entirely on elevating the in-app menu gallery inside **mobile Munch**.

This wasn’t just event coverage.
This was intentional content creation designed to showcase the craftsmanship behind every dish.

---

## The Location

Our first shoot of the 2025 season took place on a live Sunday midday event in **Topeka**, hosted at **Best Buy** on Wanamaker Rd.

With steady foot traffic, natural sunlight, and real-time customer orders rolling in, it was the perfect environment to capture authentic, high-energy food visuals.

---

## The Focus

This session was centered around one key goal:

> Capture high-quality, scroll-stopping menu photos for the in-app menu gallery feature.

The **mobile Munch** menu gallery allows users to:

* Browse full menu items visually
* Preview portion sizes and presentation
* Discover new dishes before arriving
* Make faster ordering decisions

Strong visuals directly impact engagement and we’re raising the standard for 2025 and beyond.

---

## What We Captured

* Signature entrée close-ups
* Freshly made action shots
* Detail-focused texture photography
* Full plated presentations

Every shot was taken with intentional lighting, composition, and app-display framing in mind, ensuring it looks just as good in-hand as it did in real life.

---

## Why This Matters

The in-app menu gallery is one of the most powerful features inside **mobile Munch.**

When users can *see* the food clearly and professionally presented, they are significantly more likely to:

* Visit the truck
* Try new menu items
* Share the app with friends
* Return for repeat visits

This first shoot marks a bigger push in 2025 and beyond:
Helping vendors present their menus digitally with the same pride they put into preparing each dish.

---

## Season 2025: Just Getting Started

This was only the beginning.

More live-event photoshoots were planned throughout the 2025 season across Topeka, capturing the energy, flavor, and growth of the local food truck community one dish at a time.
